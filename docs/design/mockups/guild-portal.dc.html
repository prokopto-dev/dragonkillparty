How a bid resolves
{{ t.rank }} {{ t.name }} {{ t.sub }}
Tier first. The highest tier with any bid in it wins the item. A 10-point main bid beats a 350-point alt bid, and no amount of alt bidding changes that.
Amount second. Within the winning tier, the higher bid takes it. Bids in lower tiers are never compared against it.
Blind. No number is visible until close — not the top bid, not the count in your tier. You bid what it is worth to you, once.
Second price. The winner pays the runner-up in their own tier plus one increment — or their own bid if they are the only one in it.
Holds. An accepted bid reserves the points immediately — the same points cannot win two auctions at once.
Anti-snipe. A bid inside the last two minutes pushes the close back, up to five times.
Ties. Resolved by a recorded chain: bid, then attendance, then balance, then timestamp, then a seeded roll whose seed is stored on the batch.
